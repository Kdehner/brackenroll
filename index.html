About Brackenroll

Brackenroll is a lightweight, Daggerheart-native virtual tabletop. Not a general-purpose platform bolted onto a system it wasn't built for — every feature is designed around Hope and Fear, the GM-first flow, and the way Daggerheart actually plays at the table.

Run sessions live with real-time token placement, track adversary HP in the encounter runner, roll themed 3D dice that sync across every player, and distribute handouts directly from the table. Build characters through a guided 13-step wizard and access your sheet from any device. Works for fully remote groups, physical tables with a shared screen, or anything in between.
