What You Get
⚔
GM & Player Seats
One GM owns the campaign. Players join with an invite link and link their characters. Roles are clear, access is scoped.
◉
Live Sessions
Open a session and your players see it instantly. A live indicator shows everyone when the table is active. Close it when you're done — the history stays.
🗺

📜
Homebrew System (coming soon)
Create campaign-scoped homebrew — classes, ancestries, domain cards, loot items. Version and share across campaigns. Players opt in or pin to a version.
How Campaigns Work

The GM creates a campaign and shares the invite link. Players sign in, click the link, and they're in — no codes to type, no approval queue. Each player links one or more characters to the campaign, which the GM can view during sessions.

Sessions are opened and closed by the GM. While a session is active, all players see the live indicator and can join the table. The GM controls the map, tokens, and encounter state. Players see what the GM reveals — nothing more.
